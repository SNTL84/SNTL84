# Case Study — Single Souls 30+

## MADS · Single Social 30 Meet · Surat

**Campaign:** First-ever Single Social 30 Meet  
**Audience:** Strictly 30+ singles  
**Date:** 11 September 2026 · 6 PM  
**Location:** Surat  
**Participation:** ₹699/-  
**Organizer / promoter:** MADS — Metro Ad Services  
**Registration:** [Google Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd6RmDWBQxDglKGcFGIZ0T7MI8HL2vAGVqa5hShJKb4_Nn7cg/viewform?pli=1)

## The challenge

Create awareness for a new offline social experience for 30+ singles without making it feel like a conventional dating-app or nightclub promotion.

## The campaign solution

MADS built a community-first promotional system around five minimum acquisition channels:

1. Facebook
2. Instagram
3. Meta Ads
4. Google Form registration
5. Repeated event-date promotion across every creative

The messaging was anchored around **Meet · Connect · Go Beyond**, with supporting themes such as real conversations, new connections and a bigger social circle.

## Creative system

The supplied campaign visuals use a consistent warm premium identity:

- Black, gold, cream and warm sunset tones
- A recognisable circular **Single Souls 30+** identity
- Human social scenes and conversational imagery
- Clear 30+ eligibility and event information
- Strong but friendly registration CTAs

## Conversion path

```text
Facebook / Instagram / Meta Ads
              ↓

              ↓

              ↓
      Participant confirmation
              ↓
```

## FAQ / Q&A framework

### Who can attend?
Strictly 30+ singles.

### When is the event?
11 September 2026 at 6 PM.

### Where?
Surat. Final venue information is communicated to confirmed participants.

### What is the fee?
₹699/- per participant.

### How do people register?
Through the official Google Form linked in the campaign repository.

### Is it a dating-app event?
No. It is positioned as a community-first social experience focused on meeting people, meaningful conversations and new connections.

### What should every ad communicate?
30+ eligibility, Surat, 11 September, 6 PM, ₹699 and the Google Form CTA.
